Website and newsletter privacy

This page explains the shared Plus UI Design newsletter form on Tailwind CSS Gradients. Read the Plus UI Design privacy policy for the wider policy and contact details.

Newsletter signup

When you submit the form, your email address is sent to the Plus UI Design form service and its Mailchimp newsletter audience. The service verifies the submission and may send a confirmation email before a new subscription becomes active. Submitting the form does not overwrite an existing subscription preference. Use the unsubscribe link in a newsletter to leave the mailing list.

Verification and service providers

The form uses a verification challenge served through the Plus UI Design service and Cloudflare Turnstile. The website is hosted on Vercel. These providers process technical request information needed to deliver and protect their services. The newsletter form does not request access to your design files or account passwords.

Analytics and advertising

Where enabled, this website uses analytics and Google advertising services. Their operation can involve cookies, identifiers and technical information. See the main privacy policy and any available privacy or consent controls for details. Learn about Google’s advertising technologies.

Questions and requests

Contact support@plusuidesign.com with questions about the newsletter or privacy. Product accounts, uploads and platform-specific features may be covered by additional policies shown in those workflows.
